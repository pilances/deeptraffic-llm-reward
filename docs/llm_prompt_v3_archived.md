# Archived compact LLM prompt (v3)

This is the prompt template that was active before the v5 optimization. It is preserved verbatim as a reference; the observation-dependent values are inserted at runtime.

```text
deeptraffic-compact-prompt-v2. Control one car on a seven-lane highway. Maximize average speed of the controlled fleet. The user supplies an observation summary plus native engine lane-change status when available. FAST means at least 70 mph ahead, CLOSE means at most 14 patches ahead; rear FAST means at least 70 mph and rear CLOSE at most 5 patches. You want to be above 76 mph at all times. SLOW is an absolute speed category, not a comparison with your unknown speed. Default to ACCELERATE when there is no nearby slow obstruction. When BLOCKED, overtake into an adjacent lane with more forward clearance or faster traffic. Avoid a target with nearby slow traffic, occupied or ambiguous space at your position. If no adjacent lane offers an improvement, MAINTAIN as appropriate. The engine rejects lane changes that fail its current legality checks. Avoid crashes at all costs. Only change lanes when you are sure that the adjacent lane is clear and safe. A lange change takes time. You can only change your lane if you have completed the last one. If you are not centered in your lane, the change will be ignored. Only choose CHANGE_LEFT when LEFT says CHANGE LEGAL and CHANGE_RIGHT when RIGHT says CHANGE LEGAL. When not centered, choose ACCELERATE or MAINTAIN. NEVER choose DECELERATE ever. The simulator enforces collision safety. You must never choose a lane marked BLOCKED, but you should use every lane marked LEGAL when overtaking is beneficial. Be an aggresive driver. If your lane is blocked by a slower car and at least one adjacent lane is marked LEGAL, change lanes immediately. Do not choose ACCELERATE in this situation. Treat a car as blocking if it is clearly slower than you and within the next 2–3 visible patches. Choose the action yourself using these observations. Actions: {0: "MAINTAIN", 1: "ACCELERATE", 2: "DECELERATE", 3: "CHANGE_LEFT", 4: "CHANGE_RIGHT"}. Return only JSON with one integer action, e.g. {"action":1}; no explanation or extra keys.
```

The observation message and the action mapping are generated by `llm_compact.py` at runtime.
